Good to know

Questions people ask us

Where can I get the best veg shawarma in Surat?

Shiv Paneer Shawarma is a 100% vegetarian shawarma kitchen in Surat. Every shawarma is rolled fresh to order in a whole wheat or millet wrap with about 35g of protein, and no maida, palm oil or mayonnaise. Order online for delivery, or walk in for dine-in and takeaway.

What are Shiv Paneer Shawarma's timings?

We are open every day from 5:00 pm to 11:00 pm. Evenings only — there is no lunch service. Online ordering follows the same hours.

Is Shiv Paneer Shawarma fully vegetarian?

Yes. The kitchen is pure veg — there is no meat, no egg and no separate non-veg counter. Paneer is the protein in every shawarma, bowl and kullad.

Do you deliver shawarma in Surat?

Yes. Choose Delivery on the menu page, enter your Surat address and pay by UPI. Dine-in and takeaway orders can also be paid at the counter.

Which areas of Surat do you deliver shawarma to?

From our kitchen at Madhuvan Circle we deliver to Adajan, Amroli, Bhatar, Bhathena, Bhesan Gam, Bhestan, Bhimpor, Dumas, Gaurav Path, Jahangirpura, Katargam, Mahidharpura, Majura Gate, Pal, Piplod, Sachin, Singanpore, Udhna 3 Rasta, Umra and Vesu. If you are not sure whether we reach you, enter your address at checkout and the app will tell you before you pay.

What makes this different from a regular shawarma shop?

Whole wheat and millet wraps instead of maida, no palm oil, no mayonnaise, and roughly 35g of protein per shawarma. Nothing is pre-cooked and held — each roll is made after you order it.

What is the Buy 1 Get 1 Free offer?

Add two of the same shawarma, cheese delight, kullad or bowl and the second one is taken off the bill automatically at checkout. No coupon code needed.
